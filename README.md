## 🔬 Research Projects

### ⚛️ Quantum Hardware Design

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

**[ATS Quantum Chip](https://github.com/Risav25Pokhrel/ATS-Quantum-Chip)**

Design and EPR analysis of an ATS (Asymmetrically Threaded SQUID) superconducting chip. Full pipeline: Qiskit Metal layout → HFSS 3D EM simulation → pyEPR quantization to extract qubit frequencies, anharmonicity, and dispersive shift.

`Qiskit Metal` `HFSS` `pyEPR` `Jupyter`

</td>
<td width="50%">

**[SNAIL–Fluxonium–Transmon–Fluxonium–SNAIL](https://github.com/Risav25Pokhrel/Parametric-Coupling-in-SNAIL-Fluxonium-Transmon-Fluxonium-SNAIL-)**  🥇

Modification of the MIT FTF architecture, augmenting it with SNAIL couplers for parametric three-wave mixing. Designed in Qiskit Metal, Hamiltonian dynamics simulated in QuTiP.

`Qiskit Metal` `QuTiP` `HFSS` `Parametric Gates`

</td>
</tr>
</table>

### 💻 Quantum Algorithms & Optimization

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

**[DBSCANSOLVER — Quantum VRP](https://github.com/Risav25Pokhrel/DBSCANSOLVER-Quantum-Vehicle-Routing-Problems-)**

Hybrid quantum-classical solver for the Vehicle Routing Problem. Uses recursive DBSCAN clustering to decompose large VRP instances, then solves each cluster as a QUBO on D-Wave's quantum annealer. Part of **QIntern25**.

`D-Wave` `QUBO` `DBSCAN` `scikit-learn`

</td>
<td width="50%">

**[BB84 Quantum Key Distribution](https://github.com/Risav25Pokhrel/BB84-Quantum-Key-Distribution)**

Full simulation of the BB84 QKD protocol with eavesdropper attack scenarios (`interceptAndSend`, `entangleQubit`). Capstone task for **QxQ 2023/24**.

`Cirq` `QKD` `Quantum Cryptography`

</td>
</tr>

<tr>
<td width="50%">

**[Quantum Mean Filter](https://github.com/Risav25Pokhrel/Quantum-Mean-Filter)**

Quantum implementation of the classical image mean filter using ENEQR encoding, cyclic shift circuits, and quantum addition — synthesized on the Classiq platform. Graduate course project at **University of Latvia** under **Classiq & QWorld**.

`Classiq` `ENEQR` `NEQR` `QWorld`

</td>
<td width="50%">

**[Young's Double Slit Experiment](https://github.com/Risav25Pokhrel/Young-s-double-slit-experiment)** 🏅

Quantum simulation of Young's Double Slit Experiment using Hadamard and phase gates in Qiskit, run on both AER simulator and real IBM quantum hardware. Awarded **Honorable Mention** at Future Leaders in Quantum Hackathon 2025.

`Qiskit` `IBM Quantum` `Wave-Particle Duality`

</td>
</tr>
</table>
